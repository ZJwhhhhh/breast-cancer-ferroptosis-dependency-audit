# Dependency-adjustment rules

## Claim denominator

Reports were grouped into mechanism-drug-subtype claim cells.

## Primary estimate

Within each claim cell, independent evidence was estimated by claim-specific maximum matching across canonical team and resistant-model/provenance lineages. Reports sharing a team-programme or the same documented resistant-model lineage could not automatically contribute separate independent units.

## Unresolved provenance

The primary analysis conservatively merged unresolved identical parent-cell/drug families. The deterministic sensitivity analysis split only unresolved model labels by report.

## Interpretive guardrails

- A shared cited reference is common intellectual infrastructure, not independent experimental validation.
- A common parental cell-line name does not alone prove model dependence.
- A differently named derivative does not prove independence without derivation or source information.
- Evidence levels describe replication breadth and aligned translational support, not treatment-effect magnitude or clinical efficacy.
- Claim-specific totals are not counts of unique laboratories across the entire corpus.
