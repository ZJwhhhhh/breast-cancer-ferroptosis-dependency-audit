# Evidence-level definitions

The exact operational definitions in the final audit workbook and supplementary appendix are authoritative.

- E1: single-lineage evidence.
- E2: independent replication without the higher translational alignment required for E3/E4.
- E3: multiple independent units with partial translational alignment.
- E4: independently replicated evidence with the prespecified aligned T2/T3 translational support.

Final distribution: 11 E1, 0 E2, 2 E3, and 7 E4 claims.
