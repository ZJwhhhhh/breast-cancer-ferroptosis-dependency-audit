# Reproducibility scope and honest limitations

## Fully checkable in this repository

- Eligibility and adjudication denominators.
- Dual-review agreement counts and kappa as locked in the summary.
- The 20 claim units and their report, team, model-lineage and dependency-adjusted counts.
- Primary 37-unit and sensitivity 39-unit totals.
- E1–E4 evidence-level distribution.
- Figure 1 and Figure 6 generation.
- Figure 5 redraw from the archived derived composition and residual matrices.

## Archived structural outputs

Final exported images are supplied for the VOSviewer structural analyses. Available map/network files are supplied for cited-reference co-citation and 118-Article bibliographic coupling, together with the final title–abstract thesaurus.

## Not claimed

The repository does not claim that every VOSviewer/CiteSpace/Bibliometrix figure can be reconstructed exactly from a raw licensed Web of Science export, because not every original project file and intermediate table is archived. It also does not claim to re-run the cross-layer permutation test from raw bridge-level records; the locked derived matrices and reported global statistics are preserved.
