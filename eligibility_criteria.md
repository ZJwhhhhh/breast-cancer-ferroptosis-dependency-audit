# Strict acquired-resistance eligibility

A report entered the primary evidence audit only when it evaluated ferroptosis in a breast-cancer model with a documented acquired cytotoxic-chemotherapy-resistant state or a directly aligned resistant clinical/provenance context.

Context-only reports, intrinsic drug sensitivity, general cytotoxicity, endocrine/HER2/CDK4/6/immunotherapy/radiotherapy resistance, and mechanistically adjacent reports without the required acquired-resistance claim were excluded from the strict numerator.

The operational codebook in the audit workbook is authoritative if any abbreviated statement here differs from the final adjudication rules.
